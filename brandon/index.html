Imperial Ballroom, Victoria Inn

Brandon · reception

Ihuoma and Christian moved from a church ceremony to a reception in the Imperial Ballroom at the Victoria Inn. The ballroom sits apart from the main hotel, so a large reception gets its own entrance and room to spread out.

Photo spots and neighbourhoods around Brandon.

Most Brandon couples get ready at home or at a hotel in Downtown Brandon, North Hill, the South End, Valleyview or Westridge. The city is compact, so moving from getting-ready photos to the ceremony and on to portraits rarely costs you time with your guests.

For portraits we like the Riverbank Discovery Centre trails, where the Assiniboine River gives you trees, water and long views in one short walk. Downtown, the older brick storefronts along Rosser Avenue suit couples who want an urban look. South of the city, the Brandon Hills offer rolling grassland and aspen bluffs, and in July the canola fields at the edge of town turn the whole frame yellow.

Winter weddings work here too. Fresh snow in the river valley reflects soft light back onto faces, and a short session outside followed by warm portraits indoors keeps everyone comfortable.

Brandon wedding photography FAQ.

How much does a wedding photographer cost in Brandon?

Every Brandon wedding is quoted to its date, hours and venue, so the quickest way to a number is the inquiry form or a call to +1-431-441-2676. Full-day collections cover getting ready through the first dances and include a sneak peek and a private online gallery. There is no travel fee inside Brandon, and travel within the prairies is included in most of our wedding collections.

Where are the best photo spots in Brandon?

Good choices include the Riverbank Discovery Centre trails along the Assiniboine River, the older brick storefronts on Rosser Avenue downtown, the rolling hills and aspen bluffs of the Brandon Hills south of the city, and canola fields at the edge of town in July. In winter, the snowy river valley makes a quiet, bright backdrop for portraits.

How many photos do we get and how fast?

Gallery size depends on your hours and how the day unfolds, and we give you an estimate with your quote. You receive a sneak peek within about a week of the wedding and the full edited gallery in 12 to 16 weeks, delivered as a private online gallery you can share with family and download.

Do you shoot engagement sessions in Brandon?

Yes. An engagement session runs an hour or two, usually timed for golden hour, at one or two Brandon locations you choose: the Riverbank trails, downtown, the Brandon Hills or somewhere that matters to the two of you. Booking it a few months before the wedding means you will know how we work by the time the day arrives.

Do you travel outside Brandon?

Yes. We photograph weddings across Westman, including Shilo, Carberry, Souris, Virden, Neepawa and Minnedosa, plus elopements at Clear Lake and in Riding Mountain National Park. We also cover Winnipeg and Regina. Travel within the prairies is included in most of our wedding collections, so tell us your venue and we will confirm it in your quote.

How far in advance should we book our Brandon wedding?

We take on a limited number of weddings each year so every couple gets our full attention. For summer and early-autumn Saturdays in Brandon and Westman, most couples book 12 to 18 months ahead. If your date is sooner, send an inquiry anyway; we reply within 48 hours.

Do you photograph elopements and small weddings near Brandon?

Yes. Elopements and small ceremonies, for two people or twenty, are shaped around your plans, whether that means a ceremony in town, a lakeside weekend at Clear Lake or a quiet spot in Riding Mountain National Park. We photograph the ceremony, your portraits and the people with you, and quote the coverage after a short conversation about your day. There is no travel fee inside Brandon.

How many hours of photography does a Brandon wedding need?

Our wedding collections run from about 5 hours, which covers the ceremony, family photos, your portraits and the start of the reception, up to 10 hours, which follows the whole day from getting ready to the first dances. If you get ready in Brandon and marry at a hall or farm venue out in Westman, allow time for the drive between them. Extra hours can be added before or during the wedding.

Do you help plan the photo timeline for our Brandon wedding?

Yes. Our 10-hour collection includes timeline guidance one to three weeks before the wedding. We work through when family photos happen, how much time your portraits need and where the light will be. From late September on, the sun sets around dinnertime in Brandon, so portraits often move to before the ceremony or to a short walk between dinner and speeches. Ask about timeline help for shorter collections when you book.

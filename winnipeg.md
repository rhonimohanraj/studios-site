## Winnipeg venues, and what each one does well.

### Leo Mol Sculpture Garden
Assiniboine Park · ceremony and portraits
Ashley and Mayson said their vows under the timber pergola in the Leo Mol Sculpture Garden, with guests seated along the stone walk and the reflecting pond behind them. Ivy on the pergola filters the sun, which keeps faces soft in the middle of the day.

Because the garden sits inside Assiniboine Park, their portraits moved between the bronze sculptures and the water without leaving the garden.

### The rest of Assiniboine Park
Between Tuxedo and River Heights · portraits
The Leo Mol garden sits beside the English Garden, and the Assiniboine Park Pavilion and duck pond are a short walk away, so a park wedding can hold its ceremony, family photos and couple portraits in one place. Summer brings the English Garden into full bloom; by October the park's trees turn gold.

### Downtown and riverside venues
We also photograph weddings at Winnipeg venues such as The Fort Garry Hotel, the RBC Convention Centre, Inn at the Forks, Canad Inns Destination Centre Polo Park and the Assiniboine Park Pavilion. Each suits a different kind of day.

The Fort Garry Hotel's ballrooms put the Legislative Building grounds a few blocks away for portraits. The RBC Convention Centre handles very large receptions and is a few minutes from the Exchange District's brick laneways. Inn at the Forks sits at The Forks itself, so the riverwalk is steps from the door and Esplanade Riel is a short walk. Canad Inns Polo Park is about ten minutes from Assiniboine Park, which makes a park portrait stop easy to fit between ceremony and dinner.

## Winnipeg neighbourhoods and photo spots.
The Exchange District gives you warehouse brick, cast-iron storefronts and quiet laneways around Old Market Square; it is the natural choice for couples who want an urban, editorial look. Across the Red River, St. Boniface has the cathedral facade, the French Quarter's older streets and a view back to downtown from the Esplanade Riel.

Osborne Village and Corydon bring colour, cafes and murals for relaxed engagement sessions. Crescentwood and River Heights have tree-lined streets and the grand houses along Wellington Crescent, and Tuxedo borders Assiniboine Park and the Assiniboine Forest trails for a greener, quieter frame.

Winnipeg winters suit photographs too. The river trail at The Forks, frosted Exchange streets and the Leo Mol garden under snow all photograph beautifully, and a warm lobby or cafe nearby keeps a winter session comfortable.

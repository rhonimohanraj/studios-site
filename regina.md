## Regina wedding FAQ

**Do you travel to Regina for weddings?**
Yes. Trident Studios photographs weddings in Regina and across southern Saskatchewan. Reach out with your date and venue and we'll send our travel details for the Queen City.

**Which Regina venues do you photograph at?**
We love photographing at The Hotel Saskatchewan, Conexus Arts Centre, Willow on Wascana, the Saskatchewan Legislative Building grounds, and throughout Wascana Park and Centre.

**Is Wascana Park a good spot for engagement photos?**
It's one of our favourites. Wascana Lake, the Legislative grounds, and the willows at golden hour make for a beautiful and quintessentially Regina engagement session.

**How far in advance should we book our Regina wedding?**
Because we travel for Saskatchewan weddings and take a limited number each year, we recommend reaching out 12 to 18 months ahead, especially for summer dates in Regina.

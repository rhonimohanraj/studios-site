## Where we photograph

We're based in Brandon and photograph weddings, engagements and elopements across Brandon and Westman, Winnipeg, Regina and southern Saskatchewan, and all of Manitoba and Saskatchewan — plus the occasional destination celebration. Travel within the prairies is included in most of our wedding collections; just tell us your date and venue.

City guides: [Brandon](https://www.tridentstudios.ca/brandon/) · [Winnipeg](https://www.tridentstudios.ca/winnipeg/) · [Regina](https://www.tridentstudios.ca/regina/)

## Booking ahead

We take on a limited number of weddings each year. For peak summer and early-autumn Saturdays, most couples book 12 to 18 months ahead. Trident Studios is part of Trident Event Group, so if you're also looking for a DJ, videography or a photobooth, mention it in your inquiry.

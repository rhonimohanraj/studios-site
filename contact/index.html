How to reach Trident Studios

The quickest way to check availability is our inquiry form. Share your wedding date, your venue or town, and a little about your plans. You can also email hello@tridenteventgroup.ca or call or text +1 431-441-2676. We reply to every inquiry within 48 hours, and our office hours are Monday to Friday, 9 to 5 Central time.

Where we photograph

We’re based in Brandon and photograph weddings, engagements and elopements across Brandon and Westman, Winnipeg, Regina and southern Saskatchewan, and all of Manitoba and Saskatchewan — plus the occasional destination celebration. Travel within the prairies is included in most of our wedding collections; just tell us your date and venue. City guides: Brandon, Winnipeg, Regina.

Booking ahead

We take on a limited number of weddings each year. For peak summer and early-autumn Saturdays, most couples book 12 to 18 months ahead. Trident Studios is part of Trident Event Group, so if you’re also looking for a DJ, videography or a photobooth, mention it in your inquiry.

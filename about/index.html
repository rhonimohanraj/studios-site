Behind the lens
About

Hello — we’re Trident Studios.

For more than a decade we’ve been photographing weddings, families and quiet moments across Brandon, Winnipeg, and the wide prairie country in between.

Our approach is calm and observational. We believe the best photographs come from trust — from feeling at ease in front of the camera, from being seen instead of posed. We chase honest light, we wait for the small gestures, and we put as much craft into the final edit as we do into the moment itself.

Hundreds of weddings later, our goal hasn’t changed: to give you a record of your day so beautifully made you’ll hand it down. That’s it. That’s everything.

Where we work

Our studio is at 19 Premier Avenue in Brandon, Manitoba. From there we photograph weddings across Brandon and Westman, in Winnipeg, in Regina and southern Saskatchewan, and throughout Manitoba and Saskatchewan — plus the occasional destination celebration. Travel within the prairies is included in most of our wedding collections. See our pages for Brandon, Winnipeg and Regina weddings.

What we photograph

  • Weddings — full-day, full-presence coverage with a photojournalistic eye for the moments and a portrait photographer’s craft for the details.
  • Engagements — an hour or two together before the wedding day, in soft golden light.
  • Elopements — intimate ceremonies for two, or twenty, documented with the same care as a grand celebration.
  • Editorial & lifestyle — brand stories, retreat coverage, and editorial portraiture for businesses and creatives.

Part of Trident Event Group

Trident Studios is the photography brand of Trident Event Group Inc., a Manitoba event company. Our sister brands cover the rest of the day: Trident Music for DJs, Trident Films for videography, Trident Photobooths, and Roam Content for wedding and event social content. Learn more at tridenteventgroup.ca.

We take on a limited number of weddings each year, and most couples book 12 to 18 months ahead for peak summer and early-autumn Saturdays. To check your date, send an inquiry or visit our contact page.
